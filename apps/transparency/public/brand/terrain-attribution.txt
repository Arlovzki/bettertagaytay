Tagaytay elevation contours

25 m elevation intervals, with 100 m major contours. North-up view of the
Tagaytay city extent plus a small surrounding buffer. Decorative display;
this is not an official boundary, survey or navigation map.

SRTM and GMTED2010 terrain data courtesy of the U.S. Geological Survey,
via Mapzen / AWS Terrain Tiles.
Source: https://registry.opendata.aws/terrain-tiles/
Provider attribution: https://github.com/tilezen/joerd/blob/master/docs/attribution.md
