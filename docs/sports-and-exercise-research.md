## Oval access conflict

Waze lists a 05:00 opening, with closing times of 19:00 Monday–Thursday and 21:00 Friday–Sunday. A [recent runner thread](https://www.reddit.com/r/PHRunners/comments/1w247vr/tagaytay_oval_update/) reports reopening with an asphalt surface, while a commenter describes guards clearing the venue around 18:00. An earlier [May 17, 2026 thread](https://www.reddit.com/r/PHRunners/comments/1tg2qbp/seems_this_path_will_get_busier/) reports a renovation closure. These community reports are useful leads and are not official operating rules.

Publish “Confirm today’s opening hours and access with venue staff” until the city or operator confirms them. Leave numerical hours and prices unset rather than copying conflicting map/review data. Do not label the oval 24/7, free, rubberized or fully renovated as a verified current fact.

Add a “Sports & exercise” destination under Services, with a homepage link alongside tourism. Lead with walking/running and court sports, then show cycling/skating, combat sports. Private facilities found during the initial research are excluded from the public directory. Each entry should include activities, barangay/address, a verified map link, access type, hours, fees, booking/contact details, source and last checked date. Show a short “Confirm access” state where operational details are missing. Do not manufacture map coordinates, accessibility amenities or class timetables.

Keep scheduled events separate from permanent facilities, and remove or archive past events after their date. Add the existing correction-email action to each entry.

Before publishing the directory, resolve three issues in existing content:

1. The tourism page says the velodrome is “Open for training and events.” Replace this with wording that asks visitors to confirm training or event access.
2. The public-facilities guide calls TSAC the main complex and supplies an unsourced reservation workflow. Confirm its current name, relationship to the combat center, operator and booking process. Do not merge names based on similarity.
3. Use the current city homepage's main switchboard, (046) 888-9500, as a general enquiry route. The old directory and some secondary official pages retain older telephone numbers; neither proves a direct sports-facility contact.
