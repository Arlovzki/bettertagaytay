# Downloadable Forms and Documents

Official forms and documents from the Tagaytay City Government. These are the same forms available at the City Hall. Download, fill out, and bring them to the relevant office to save time.

Last link check: **5 November 2026**. The building permit, fencing permit, and completion forms are labelled 2026 on the official site. For building and related permit forms, follow its instruction to print back to back using the same colour.

## Where to Submit

Bring completed forms to the relevant office at **Tagaytay City Hall**, Akle St., Kaybagal South, Tagaytay City.

For inquiries: **(046) 888-9500** (connecting all offices) or **(046) 413-1220**

> All downloadable forms are sourced from the [Official Tagaytay City Government Website](https://www.tagaytay.gov.ph/downloads.html). Check there for the most up-to-date versions.
