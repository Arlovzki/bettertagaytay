# Find Scholarships in Tagaytay

Start with the **City College of Tagaytay (CCT) Scholarship Office** for the college’s scholarship and student-support options. Application, admission, and grant rules are separate decisions; confirm each with the responsible college office.

## Where to ask

- Scholarship and subsidy inquiries: [scholarship@citycollegeoftagaytay.edu.ph](mailto:scholarship@citycollegeoftagaytay.edu.ph)
- Admission inquiries: [admissions@citycollegeoftagaytay.edu.ph](mailto:admissions@citycollegeoftagaytay.edu.ph)
- College telephone: **(046) 482-6840**

These contacts appear in the college’s [official directory](https://citycollegeoftagaytay.edu.ph/homepage/about).

## Steps

1. Ask whether the current application cycle is open and which options fit your applicant or enrollment status.
2. Request the current eligibility rules, deadline, application method, and document checklist.
3. Prepare the documents requested by the college and submit through its designated office or official application channel.
4. Keep your application reference and follow the college’s assessment instructions.
5. Read the coverage, retention conditions, and any agreement before accepting a grant.

## Before you apply

The college publishes information about fee coverage, grant duration, and retention on its [scholarship page](https://citycollegeoftagaytay.edu.ph/homepage/scholarship), and applicant-specific documents on its [admissions page](https://citycollegeoftagaytay.edu.ph/homepage/admissions).

Ask the Scholarship Office for the current deadline and award conditions. For a separate city or national scholarship, use the requirements and application channel in that program’s current official announcement.
