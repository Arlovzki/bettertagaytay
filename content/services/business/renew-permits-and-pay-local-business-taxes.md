# Renew a Business Permit

Renew your Tagaytay business permit through BPLO and pay the assessed local business taxes.

## Renewal period

The city’s Business Permit and Licensing page lists **1–20 January** for annual renewal. Check current city announcements for any change or extension.

## Requirements

The published renewal list includes a copy of the previous income tax return, barangay and sanitary clearances, fire documents, and property-tax or lease documents. Additional permits depend on the business. Bring your previous permit and ask BPLO for the current renewal checklist.

## Steps

1. Confirm the filing location, current checklist, and renewal schedule with BPLO.
2. Submit your application and records for verification and assessment.
3. Complete any clearance updates the office requires.
4. Pay the assessed taxes and charges at the designated city cashier. Keep the official receipt.
5. Claim the renewed permit and any plate or sticker issued for your business.

## Fees and processing

The assessment depends on your business and declared receipts/sales. Ask BPLO to confirm charges, payment options, and the release date. Do not rely on a fixed processing estimate before document review.

Source: [Tagaytay Business Permit and Licensing](https://www.tagaytay.gov.ph/business.html) and [2025 Citizen’s Charter, printed pages 100–101](https://www.tagaytay.gov.ph/pdf/citizenscharter.pdf#page=101).
